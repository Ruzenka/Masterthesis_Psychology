This folder contains the materials used in a master's thesis with the title Altruism and parochialism in meat-consumption profiles: Conflict intensity and identification with dietary and mammalian ingroup.

## Files

- **[veg_an_survey.pdf](veg_an_survey.pdf)** – Questionnaire for veg*an participants.
- **[meat_consumer_survey.pdf](meat_consumer_survey.pdf)** – Questionnaire for meat-consuming participants.
- **[high_conflict_intensity_vignette.png](high_conflict_intensity_vignette.png)** – Experimental vignette for the high-conflict-intensity condition.
- **[low_conflict_intensity_vignette.png](low_conflict_intensity_vignette.png)** – Experimental vignette for the low-conflict-intensity condition.

## Study Procedure

Participants were recruited via Prolific and prescreened according to their self-reported dietary profile. They were directed to the corresponding questionnaire in SoSci Survey, where their dietary profile was verified again.

Conflict intensity was experimentally manipulated through a text-based vignette. Participants were randomly assigned to either a high- or low-conflict-intensity condition.

The questionnaires subsequently assessed identification with the dietary ingroup, identification with mammals, altruistic preferences toward an anonymous other, parochial preferences involving the dietary ingroup and outgroup, and altruistic preferences toward mammals. Demographic information was collected at the end of the survey.
