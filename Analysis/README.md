This folder contains the R scripts used for data preprocessing and statistical analyses in a master's thesis titled Altruism and parochialism in meat-consumption profiles: Conflict intensity and identification with dietary and mammalian ingroup.

## Files

- [**Preprocessing.R**](Preprocessing.R) – Data cleaning and preparation for statistical analyses.
- [**Descriptive_analysis.R**](Descriptive_analysis.R) – Descriptive statistics of the study sample and variables.
- [**Confirmatory_analysis.R**](Confirmatory_analysis.R) – Confirmatory analyses testing the preregistered hypotheses.
- [**Exploratory_analysis_for_investment_dimension.R**](Exploratory_analysis_for_investment_dimension.R) – Exploratory analyses of the self-investment dimension of social identification.
- [**Exploratory_analysis_ for_definition_dimension.R**](Exploratory_analysis_%20for_definition_dimension.R) – Exploratory analyses of the self-definition dimension of social identification.
