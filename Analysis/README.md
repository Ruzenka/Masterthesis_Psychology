This folder contains the R scripts used for data preprocessing and statistical analyses in a master's thesis titled Altruism and parochialism in meat-consumption profiles: Conflict intensity and identification with dietary and mammalian ingroup.

## Files

- [**Preprocessing.R**](Preprocessing.R) – Data cleaning and preparation for statistical analyses.
- [**Descriptive_analysis.R**](Descriptive_analysis.R) – Descriptive statistics of the study sample and variables.
- [**Confirmatory_analysis.R**](Confirmatory_analysis.R) – Confirmatory analyses testing the preregistered hypotheses.
- [**Exploratory_analysis_for_investment_dimension.R**](Exploratory_analysis_for_investment_dimension.R) – Exploratory analyses of the self-investment dimension of social identification.
- [**Exploratory_analysis_ for_definition_dimension.R**](Exploratory_analysis_%20for_definition_dimension.R) – Exploratory analyses of the self-definition dimension of social identification.

## Data analysis

The preprocessing script prepared the raw survey data, while the descriptive analysis script summarized the sample characteristics and main study variables. The confirmatory analyses tested four preregistered hypotheses: (H1) whether higher conflict intensity leads to higher meat-consumption-based parochialism; (2) whether higher identification with one's dietary ingroup is associated with higher parochialism; (3) whether meat-avoiders show higher identification with their mammalian ingroup than meat-consumers; and (4) whether meat-avoiders show higher altruism toward a mammalian ingroup recipient than meat-consumers. Additional exploratory analyses investigated whether the individual components and dimensions of mammalian identification differ between dietary groups and whether they are associated with altruistic preferences toward mammals. Specifically, separate analyses examined the self-investment dimension (solidarity, satisfaction, and centrality) and the self-definition dimension (individual self-stereotyping and perceived ingroup homogeneity).
